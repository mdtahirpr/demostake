Common questions

FAQ

What is fractional real estate investing? ↓
Fractional investing lets you buy a percentage of a property instead of the whole thing. You earn rental income and price appreciation proportional to your ownership share. DemoStake handles all the legal structure so your shares are formally registered.
How are returns calculated? ↓
Returns come from two sources: rental income (paid monthly) and capital appreciation (realised when the property is sold). The projected annual return shown on each listing combines both, but actual returns may vary based on occupancy and market conditions.
Can I withdraw my money early? ↓
You can sell your shares on our secondary marketplace at any time, subject to buyer availability. We also run bi-annual exit windows where DemoStake facilitates liquidity for all investors who wish to exit.
Who can invest on DemoStake? ↓
DemoStake is open to investors from 186+ countries. You need to be 18 or older and pass our KYC (Know Your Customer) identity verification. Some restrictions may apply based on your country of residence.
What are the fees? ↓
DemoStake charges a management fee of 15% of rental income to cover property management, tenant services, and maintenance. There are no hidden fees, no subscription charges, and no fees to create an account.
